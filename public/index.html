A plain-English guide for people who already work on projects and want that experience recognised.

About the qualification

The Certificate IV in Project Management Practice (BSB40920) is a nationally recognised AQF level 4 qualification, also called the Certificate IV in Project Management or Cert 4 in Project Management. Through recognition of prior learning (RPL), 3CIR assesses the skills you already use in project work, and Asset College issues the qualification.

What the units cover

BSB40920 has 9 units, made up of 3 core and 6 electives. The core units cover project scope management, project time management and project quality management. Three electives come from the project management group, and the other three come from the project management and transferable skills groups, or from a Certificate IV or higher where a unit is not listed.

Who can apply

Australian citizens and residents living in Australia can apply. No entry requirements are listed for BSB40920 on training.gov.au. Evidence from paid work, self-employment or volunteering can count, as long as you can show you already use the skills in each unit. Project records you helped create usually suit the core units.
